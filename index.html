The problem

You will forget much of what you read this week. Unless you learn it differently.

What we read fades fast when we never practice it. More effort isn’t the answer. A better method is.

It isn’t effort. It’s method.

The study habits that feel productive are the ones that work least.

A landmark 2013 review rated ten common study techniques by how much they helped across many studies. Rereading and highlighting came out near the bottom. Testing yourself and spacing your practice came out on top.

What most of us do

  • Rereading Low utility
  • Highlighting Low utility
  • Summarizing Low utility

What actually works

  • Practice testing High utility
  • Spaced practice High utility
  • Self-explanation Moderate utility

Dunlosky, Rawson, Marsh, Nathan & Willingham (2013), “Improving Students’ Learning With Effective Learning Techniques,” Psychological Science in the Public Interest. “Spaced practice” is the review’s “distributed practice”.

Questions

Before you join.

The short answers. The film shows the rest.

When can I use Veritas?

Veritas launches online in December 2026. You sign in with your own account, and your library, notes and progress go with you. Join the waitlist and we’ll email you when it’s ready.

What can I bring into it?

PDFs, EPUB books, Word documents, web pages, photos and scans, or text you paste in. Everything goes into one library with shelves and collections, and chapters are found for you.

Does it really read in my own voice?

Yes. You read six short passages aloud once, about two minutes, and Veritas builds your voice from them. You can also choose a natural preset voice, and change speed any time.

How much time does it take each day?

You decide. In your plan you set how many books to master a week or a month and which days you study, and it shows the minutes a day that takes. Today lists each task with its time before you start. A chapter’s knowledge check is about 12 questions, about 12 minutes.

Who writes my notes?

You do. Notes start as a blank bullet for each chapter and stay entirely yours. Veritas never writes or inserts notes for you, because writing them is part of how you learn.

How does Veritas decide what I review?

Every chapter ends with a knowledge check. What you miss becomes a weak point. Weak points come back in practice and in your daily review, sooner while you’re still getting them wrong and less often once you know them. Your plan shows what to do today and when each book will be mastered.

Is it just flashcards?

No. There are six ways to practice: written answers, multiple choice, drawing from memory, teaching it back out loud, flashcards and games. A short check of how well you do in each mode sets where you start, and Veritas recommends a method from your results.

I already use a read-aloud or flashcard app. Why switch?

Keep them if they work for you. Veritas is for when you want listening, notes, checks and review on the same text, so what you miss is what comes back.
